Our signature event · 21st year

Lawn Party by the Bay

Saturday, December 5, 2026 · 12:00–2:30 PM · Ken Thompson Park, City Island, 1700 Ken Thompson Parkway, Sarasota. More than 800 guests, tastings from up to 30 local restaurants, fine wine, craft beer, live music and the 50/50 raffle, all for a wonderful cause. Hosted by the Kiwanis Club of Longboat Key. This year's proceeds benefit the Children's Guardian Fund, which cares for children and young adults in foster care in Sarasota, Manatee and DeSoto Counties.

The Lawn Party's impact

800+ guests

Neighbors, families and friends from across Sarasota, Manatee and Longboat Key, every year.

Up to 30 restaurants

Tastings from our area's favorite local restaurants, plus fine wine and craft beer.

$123,900 in 2025

$34,000+ 50/50 raffle

Half to the winner, half to children in need. In 2025, the winners donated 100% of the prize back.

Restaurants: showcase your signature dish

What we ask

About 500 to 600 small tastes of your featured dish. Arrive by 11:30 AM for setup; guests arrive at noon and the event wraps up by 2:30 PM. We provide the tents, tables, plates, napkins, utensils and electricity.

What you receive

Recognition in all our marketing, including social media, our websites, the event brochure and email blasts to over 5,000 people; print ads, flyers and posters around town; and four complimentary tickets.
