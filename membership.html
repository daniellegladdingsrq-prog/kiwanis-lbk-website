Membership

Join the Kiwanis Club of Longboat Key

Members are neighbors who give their time and talent to the children of our community. We meet for breakfast on Thursday mornings at The Lazy Lobster, run the Pancake Breakfast and the Lawn Party by the Bay, and support local charities that serve children. New members are always welcome.

Membership · Kiwanis year October 1, 2026 – September 30, 2027

How to join

Become a Kiwanis member and support our local children. Come to one of our breakfast meetings as our guest, meet the members, and join us.

  1. Download the form for your membership level below.
  2. Fill it in and choose your payment: a check payable to Kiwanis Club of Longboat Key, Inc., or a credit card (processed securely through Zeffy).
  3. Hand it to our Treasurer, Jeff McKee, at any meeting, or mail it to the address on the form.

Why join?

  • Make a lasting difference for children right here in our community.
  • Meet neighbors and local business owners over breakfast.
  • Help run events the whole island enjoys, from the Pancake Breakfast to the Lawn Party by the Bay.
  • Hear from guest speakers about the people and work shaping Longboat Key.
