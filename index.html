Serving the children of our community

Neighbors on Longboat Key, working for local kids.

The Kiwanis Club of Longboat Key is a volunteer service club, part of Kiwanis International. We meet for breakfast on Thursday mornings at The Lazy Lobster, raise money at events like the Pancake Breakfast and the Lawn Party by the Bay, and put it to work for children here at home.

Where the money goes

Our causes

The Miracle League of Manasota

Our charitable partner. The Miracle League gives children and adults with disabilities the chance to play baseball on a specially designed, fully accessible field in Sarasota, with seasons every spring and fall. Proceeds from the club's pancake breakfast funded a $3,500 gift to the league in 2026.

Visit The Miracle League of Manasota →

The Children's Guardian Fund

The 2026 beneficiary of the Lawn Party by the Bay. The fund supports local children in need. The Lawn Party beneficiary is chosen each year.

Visit the Children's Guardian Fund →

Lawn Party tickets and sponsorships at LBKLawnParty.org →

Family Promise of Sarasota-Manatee

The club's pancake breakfasts have also supported Family Promise, which helps local families with children who are facing homelessness.

Visit Family Promise of Sarasota-Manatee →
