Serving Kids in Need, Since 1972

Neighbors on Longboat Key, working for local kids.

The Kiwanis Club of Longboat Key brings neighbors together to change children's lives, right here at home. Through friendship, service and community events like the Lawn Party by the Bay and our Pancake Breakfast, we raise the support that gives local children in need a brighter start and a reason to believe someone is in their corner.

We are part of Kiwanis International, and we share its purpose: making lasting differences in the lives of children. We meet for breakfast on the second and fourth Thursday of each month at The Lazy Lobster, and guests are always welcome.
