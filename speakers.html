Second and fourth Thursdays

Meetings & guest speakers

The Kiwanis Club of Longboat Key meets for breakfast on the second and fourth Thursday of each month at The Lazy Lobster, 5350 Gulf of Mexico Drive, Longboat Key. Breakfast is at 8:30 and the meeting starts at 9:00. Guests are always welcome, and breakfast is $20 for guests. Dates occasionally change for holidays, so please check Upcoming events or RSVP before you come.

RSVP to Michael Garey Directions Upcoming events

What to expect

A Thursday morning with the club

8:30 · Breakfast

Coffee, breakfast and conversation with neighbors at The Lazy Lobster.

9:00 · Call to order

The Pledge of Allegiance, a song, an invocation, and a welcome to members and guests.

Guest speaker

A leader from our island or our region talks about the work being done for our community and its children.

Club business

Updates on our service projects and fundraisers, from the Pancake Breakfast to the Lawn Party by the Bay, and the Ace in the Hole drawing.

Please RSVP to Michael Garey at michael@lazylobsterlbk.com so the kitchen can plan. Bring a friend!
